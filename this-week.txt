Reading: Matthew 8:23-27

Notes:
## Who is this man?
The disciples have watched Jesus teach and heal, yet the storm shows them how little they have understood.
- **The storm is real.** Following Jesus does not take us out of hard places.
- **Jesus is present.** He is in the boat with them the whole way through.
- **His word is enough.** One sentence, and the wind and waves are still.

## To take home
- Where do you most need to remember that Jesus is with you this week?
- Who could you share that with?
